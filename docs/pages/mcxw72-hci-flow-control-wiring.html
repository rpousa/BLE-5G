Rework sheet · FRDM-MCXW72 BLE boards

HCI UART with RTS/CTS on LPUART0

The HCI link of both BLE boards has moved off the MCU-Link virtual COM port (LPUART1, no flow control) onto LPUART0 with hardware flow control. A Raspberry Pi Pico per board does the bridging. Both Picos plug into claudepc2, the UE PC, and connect to their board's Arduino headers with jumper wires. No board cuts or soldering were needed.

Check list · 9 Nov

Do any pins need changing?

BLE1 · no change

All four needed wires are right

TX, RX, GND and the board's RTS on J1-5 all work. During a burst, J1-5 rises at Pico GP2 (high in 35 of 83 samples), and 300 back-to-back 36-byte commands all got a reply.

BLE2 · fixed 9 Nov

Pico pin 4 (GP2) is now on J1-5

The GP2 wire had been on a pin that is always low. After it moved to J1-5, 36-byte HCI commands get a reply, BLE2's Bluetooth link to claudepc comes up and n3iwue2 has a PDU session.

Both · optional

The J2-9 wire (Pico pin 5, GP3) is not used

Firmware v11 turns the board's CTS input off, so the board sends whenever it wants, and the Pico's 8 KB receive buffer keeps up at 1 Mbaud. The wire can stay or come off. On BLE1 it reads as held low, so J2-9 is probably not PTA18 on this board revision.

Everything else · no change

MCU-Link cables stay

Leave both boards' USB-C (MCU-Link) cables where they are. BLE1's is on claudepc2, BLE2's on claudepc. They power the boards and carry flashing and the isp recovery command.

How it connects

Four USB cables in total. The MCU-Link cables stay as they are, for power, flashing and the isp command; BLE2's happens to be on claudepc. The two Pico cables carry HCI, both on claudepc2.

Changes

What has to change on the board

No rework

No cuts, jumpers or solder bridges

RX, TX and RTS of LPUART0 are already routed to J1. Four jumper wires per board are enough.

Shared pin

The green LED now shows RTS

PTA19 drives both J1-5 and LED_G. The green LED is on while the board can take more bytes and flickers off when it asks the Pico to pause. hci_bb v11 no longer drives it as a status light.

Voltage

3.3 V on both sides

The board's headers run at VBOARD = 3.3 V, and the Pico's GPIO is 3.3 V. No level shifting is needed.

Checked

What the schematic questions turned out to be

  • PTA16/PTA17 (LIN_RX/LIN_TX in the SDK) carry 1 Mbaud cleanly, so nothing on the board loads them.
  • J2-9 reads as held low on BLE1, so it is probably not PTA18 on this board revision. Since v11 does not use CTS, it no longer matters.
  • RTS edges through the LED_G resistor are clean at 1 Mbaud.
Changes

Pico bridge firmware

Each Pico runs MicroPython 1.24.1 and a 60-line main.py that passes bytes between its USB serial port and UART0: 1 Mbaud, GP0 TX, GP1 RX, GP2 CTS (hardware), 8 KB receive buffer. It starts by itself 2 s after power-up.

  • The bridge ignores Ctrl-C. A fixed escape sequence stops it and drops to the REPL, which pico/mpy_run.py uses for tests; machine.reset() starts the bridge again.
  • Each Pico has a stable name, /dev/serial/by-id/usb-MicroPython_Board_in_FS_mode_<id>-if00, which the host scripts map to BLE1 and BLE2.
Changes

Firmware: hci_bb v11 (flashed 9 Nov)

HCI moved to LPUART0 at 1 Mbaud with the SDK's own switch, gBoardUseUart0HwFlowControl. The board's receive RTS is on and its transmit CTS is off. Image ~/nxp/images/hci_bb_isp_v11.bin, sha256 0b7ce4ba…, on both boards. Both boards' earlier images are backed up in ~/nxp/backups.

/* examples/_boards/frdmmcxw72/wireless_examples/ble_controller/hci_bb/app_preinclude.h */
#define BOARD_APP_UART_INSTANCE      0U        /* LPUART0: PTA16 RX, PTA17 TX, PTA19 RTS_b  */
#define gBoardUseUart0HwFlowControl  1
#define BOARD_APP_UART_BAUDRATE      1000000U

/* hci_bb.c, first run: after main() has called PLATFORM_InitLcl() */
BOARD_InitPinLPUART0_RTS();                    /* PTA19 back to LPUART0_RTS_b (Alt3)       */
MODIR = (MODIR & ~(TXCTSE | RTSWATER)) | RXRTSE | RTSWATER(4);
  • The bug that cost the most time: the shared main.c calls PLATFORM_InitLcl() after the board's pin setup. It switches PTA18–20 to the radio's debug outputs (RF_GPO, Alt6), which silently takes RTS off J1-5. Without RTS, every HCI command longer than about 12 bytes was lost at 1 Mbaud. v11 sets the pin back.
  • Recovery without SW3: the VCOM (LPUART1, 115200) now only listens for isp and answers ISP before jumping to the ROM bootloader, within 0.1 s of a reset. pico/vcom_isp.py sends it, then board.sh flash works as before. HCI command 0xFDB1 still does the same.
  • The H4 resync timer now measures the gap from the last byte read, and the main loop drains the receive ring too. Diagnostic report 0xFDB2 carries the resync count; it stays at 0 under load.
Changes

Host: claudepc2

# before, over the MCU-Link VCOM, flow control off
btattach -N -P h4 -S 115200 -B /dev/serial/by-id/usb-NXP_..._MCU-LINK_..._3VWLILYT2A1AK-if02

# now, over the Pico (ble_attach.sh picks the port with hci_probe.py --port)
btattach -N -P h4 -S 1000000 -B /dev/ttyACM4   # = usb-MicroPython_Board_in_FS_mode_e661640843676727-if00
  • ~/nxp/hci_probe.py maps board serials to Pico serials (PICOS) and tries the Pico first, then the VCOM. --port prints the tty. ble_attach.sh uses it. The old copies are kept as *.v10bak.
  • ble_watchdog.sh is unchanged. It can't hardware-reset BLE2 from claudepc2, because BLE2's MCU-Link is on claudepc. It still re-attaches it.
Measured · 9 Nov

What it fixed

BLE1

1000 HCI commands in a row: all answered, 5.6 ms average round trip (worst 14 ms). 300 back-to-back 36-byte commands, about 11 KB in one go: all 300 answered, no H4 resyncs. n3iwue1 registered over it (12.1.1.118) and pings to ext-dn pass.

BLE2, after the wire moved

Long commands answered, Bluetooth link up, n3iwue2 registered (12.1.1.122).

Still open

Under 1000-byte pings on both links, round trips grew to 3–15 s, and BLE1 stopped answering HCI once (kernel: command tx timeout); the watchdog reset it. Under investigation.

Won't change much

BLE throughput. The air link, not the UART, is the limit (about 13 kbit/s clean per link). The gain is reliability. Whether the old PC→board hang is gone still needs a long uplink load test.
