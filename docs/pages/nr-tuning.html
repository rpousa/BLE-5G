What each step changed

cumulative: each step keeps the ones before it · steps 1–3 and 5 kept, step 4 reverted
  1. 0As it was

    TDD 7 DL + special + 2 UL slots in 5 ms, min_rxtxtime 6, OAI default ulsch_max_frame_inactivity (10 frames). The gNB bridge polls three UE boards each uplink slot.

    LiDAR 643 ms · detections 441 ms · 49 slots/s, 50 % of polls timed out

  2. 1Poll only nrUE1 and nrUE2

    gnb_bridge.py --ues 1,2 (nrlink.sh passes it on the core PC). UE3's board became Bluetooth board BLE1, but the bridge still polled UE3 every uplink slot and waited out two 25 ms timeouts.

    LiDAR 212 ms (was 643) · detections 120 ms (was 441) · 220 slots/s

  3. 2Grant the uplink without being asked

    ulsch_max_frame_inactivity = 0 in vnf.conf: a UE without a grant for one TDD period gets one, so uplink data skips the scheduling request → grant round trip.

    LiDAR 188 ms (was 212) · detections 102 ms (was 120) · 217 slots/s

  4. 3min_rxtxtime 6 → 4

    The minimum gap, in slots, from a grant to its PUSCH and from data to its acknowledgement. 2 was tried twice: the nrUEs never attach (no uplink at all), so 4 is the floor here.

    LiDAR 155 ms (was 188) · detections 82 ms (was 102) · 201 slots/s

  5. 4TDD 5 DL + special + 4 UL — reverted

    nrofDownlinkSlots 7 → 5, nrofUplinkSlots 2 → 4, same 5 ms period. 3 DL + 6 UL was tried first: random access fails (Msg3 never received). 5 + 4 attaches but is slower: every uplink slot costs a poll round over the board link, so the slot rate fell from about 200 to 145 a second and LiDAR went from 155 to 170 ms. Bluetooth's LiDAR was slower in that run too, for another reason: BLE1 had reconnected and was left at its 45 ms opening interval (see the Bluetooth note below). Back to 7 + 2.

    LiDAR 170 ms (was 155) · detections 85 ms (was 82) · 156 slots/s

  6. 5Leave empty downlink slots off the air

    gnb_bridge.py --skip-empty: a downlink-only slot with nothing in it (no SSB, DCI or transport block) is not broadcast. The nrUE already runs any slot it did not hear when the next one arrives, as it does for a slot lost over the air. About half of all slots are skipped; a broadcast took 2.0 ms. Compared with the step 3 setup re-measured just before.

    LiDAR 144 ms (was 153) · detections 70 ms (was 80) · 248 slots/s

Why slots did not need to get smaller

The air interface is emulated. For each slot, the gNB's PNF hands the slot to the bridge. The bridge broadcasts it over the 2.4 GHz board link and, in uplink slots, polls each UE board in turn. Real time per slot comes from that exchange, not from the 0.5 ms the 30 kHz numerology nominally gives a slot. So a shorter slot or TDD period does not help; less waiting in each exchange and fewer slot round trips per packet do.

The first step was the big one. A missing UE cost two 25 ms poll timeouts in every uplink slot, about two thirds of all wall time. Removing it raised the slot rate about fourfold. The scheduler changes after it each removed a few slots from every packet's path.

Where the gains stop. min_rxtxtime below 4 and a 3 DL / 6 UL pattern both stop the nrUEs from attaching. More uplink slots do attach, but each one costs a poll round, so they slow the slot rate more than they save. Leaving empty downlink slots off the air (step 5) took the slot rate to about 240 a second. What remains is mostly the uplink slots: 10.4 ms each, a broadcast plus a poll round trip to each board. Polling both UEs in one exchange is the next step.

A Bluetooth note. During step 4, BLE1 reconnected and its request for a 15 ms connection interval got no answer from the core's adapter, so it stayed at the 45 ms it opens with (LiDAR about 265 ms instead of 170). A reconnect fixed it, and ble_ip_link.py now retries the request and reconnects if it never gets an answer. Steps 0–3 and 5 had BLE1 at 15 ms.

Where NR and Bluetooth stand now. On the same messages, NR LiDAR (144 ms median) now beats Bluetooth's (175 ms). For detections, Bluetooth is still faster (50 against 70 ms): its connection event comes every 15 ms, and an NR packet still needs several emulated slots end to end.
