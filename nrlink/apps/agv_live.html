AGV Testbed

One AGV on a 48 × 30 m factory floor. Its LiDAR scans go out over NR (nrUE1, nrlink + OAI nFAPI) and Bluetooth LE (n3iwue1, through the N3IWF) at the same moment. Its camera detections and pose go out the same way on nrUE2 and n3iwue2. Each checkpoint pole carries both radios; the AGV stops there to have its position corrected, and steers on its LiDAR in between.

How it is measured

Same data, same moment. The AGV simulation on the UE PC builds each message once and hands the identical bytes to two forwarders, one inside an nrUE's network namespace and one inside an n3iwue's. Each copy carries the message's sequence number and send time, so the edge in the data network sees every message twice and compares the two copies directly.

Delay is one way, on the core PC's clock: the UE PC's clock offset is measured every 30 s over the LAN. A copy not seen 10 s after it was sent counts as lost. Sync answer is the time from the AGV stopping at a checkpoint to the edge's position fix arriving back over that path; the AGV drives on with the first one.

The radio channel is simulated from the AGV's position, and the delays and losses are measured on the real boards, which sit on a bench. With “Simulated fades drop messages” on, a copy is not sent while its simulated link is out.
