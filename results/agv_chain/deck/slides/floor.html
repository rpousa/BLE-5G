03 · THE SCENARIO

A cold store the gNB cannot reach

−80 dBm

gNB signal almost everywhere on the floor

−118 dBm

inside the shielded cold store: the NR link is lost

−100 dBm

below it, an AGV stops using its own UE and goes over the sidelink

The AGVs drive a loop as a platoon. The one inside the store reaches the network through an AGV still outside.

AGV Sidelink Testbed
The boards sit on one desk, so the controller applies a path-loss model by position (n = 3.5, up to 32 dB more past the store's door) and stops and starts each AGV's UE as a lost and regained connection would.
