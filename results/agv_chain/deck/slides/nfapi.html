02 · 5G OVER THE BOARDS

The boards replace the radio, so time slows down

The gNB is split at nFAPI. Every slot the PNF would hand to an RF PHY goes over the boards instead; the nrUEs’ MAC runs as with a real PHY.

AGV Sidelink Testbed
The PNF's slot handler and the nrUE have a hook (nrlink) that sends the FAPI contents over the boards. Uplink: the gNB board polls every UE board in every UL slot. The PNF's time server sets the slot clock, so all NR timers follow it. Tuning: poll only attached UEs, skip empty slots, UL grants every frame, min_rxtxtime 4: LiDAR one-way p50 643 → 144 ms.
