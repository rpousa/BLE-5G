04 · THE RELAY

Another AGV relays it, as 3GPP specifies

01 DISCOVER

Hear the relays

AGVs with a good gNB signal announce themselves every 0.5 s. The remote picks the strongest above −90 dBm.

02 LINK

Secure PC5 link

PC5-S set-up with keys per link; the traffic goes in ESP. About 0.7 s.

03 ADDRESS

IP from the relay

DHCPv4 on the link; the relay reports the remote to the SMF.

04 ROUTE

Routed and NATed

The relay’s kernel sends the remote’s IP into its relay PDU session.

Layer-3 UE-to-network relay: TS 23.304, 24.554, 38.304. The remote keeps its relay until its own NR link is back.

AGV Sidelink Testbed
Thresholds as SIB12 would carry them: relay while Uu RSRP is between −95 and −31 dBm, remote below −100 dBm, SD-RSRP above −90 dBm, 2 dB to reselect. The Gateways tab of the page shows the full rule.
