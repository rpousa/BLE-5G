AGV Sidelink Testbed
Core PC: the edge server that collects the AGVs’ data, free5GC, the OAI gNB split into VNF and PNF, and gnb_bridge to the gNB board. Four FRDM-MCXW72 boards replace the RF front end: one for the gNB, one per AGV. UE PC: the AGV controller (driving, positioning, LiDAR), three OAI nrUEs, six OAI sidelink UEs and their bridges to the boards. The AGVs are software; the radio, the 5G stack and the core are real.
